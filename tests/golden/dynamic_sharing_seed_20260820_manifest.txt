The legacy shared result deliberately preserves SharedLine.cpp behavior:
candidate capacities are charged as occupied spares, allocation order is
A/B/C/D, and all 256 configuration combinations are tested.  A future
physical-line ledger may intentionally differ, but it must be reported as a
new-policy result rather than silently changing this golden baseline.
